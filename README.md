
# Bank Customer Churn Analysis | Power BI 📊

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents a Power BI dashboard designed to explore bank customer churn, analyze customer behavior, identify potential risk factors, and support data-driven business decisions.

The report combines customer analysis, interactive visualizations, and business-oriented recommendations.

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Data Modeling
- Data Visualization
- Business Intelligence

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Home
The landing page of the Power BI report, providing navigation to the different analysis sections.

### 2. Overview
A high-level overview of customer churn and customer-related metrics.

### 3. Balance
An analysis focused on customer account balances and their relationship with churn.

### 4. Drivers
An exploration of customer characteristics and potential factors associated with churn.

### 5. High Risk
A dedicated section for investigating customer groups that may require additional retention attention.

### 6. Recommendations
A section presenting business-oriented recommendations based on the analysis.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Understand customer churn patterns.
- Explore relationships between customer behavior and churn.
- Identify customer groups for further risk investigation.
- Present analytical findings through interactive dashboards.
- Support customer retention discussions with data.
